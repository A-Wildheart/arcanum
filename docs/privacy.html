Privacy

Updated 15 November 2026

Arcanum is a tool for writing and building visual novels. This page covers the website, the studio you open from it, the single-file download and the desktop app.

We collect nothing

Arcanum has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It never sends your stories, pictures, recordings or anything else to us or to anyone else.

Where your work is kept

  • On the website and in the single file: your projects are stored in your browser, on your computer. Clearing the browser's site data deletes them, so save a project file (File ▸ Save project file) to keep a copy.
  • In the desktop app: your projects are stored in the app's own folder on your computer (Help ▸ Where are my projects saved? shows it). Uninstalling the app keeps them.

What the website loads

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. The pages and the online studio load their typefaces from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it sends them. The download section asks GitHub which installers are available. The desktop app saves its typefaces on your computer the first time it is online and asks the web for nothing after that.

Microphone

The studio asks to use the microphone only if you choose to record a voice line. Recordings stay in your project on your computer.

Files you share

When you export or publish a story, the file goes only where you send it. A story you publish can include features you switch on for your readers, such as sending a code to an address you choose; those go between your readers and you, never to us.

Children

Arcanum does not collect information from anyone, including children.

The iPad and iPhone app

The App Store app has its own privacy policy, shown inside the app under Help ▸ Privacy policy and on its support site.

Changes

If this page changes, the new version carries a new date.
