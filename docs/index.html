Turn your comics into visual novels.

Arcanum is a free studio for writers and artists. Plan your story on cards, write it, bring in your art, stage each scene, and publish a branching story anyone can read.

Open Arcanum in your browser Download for your computer

No account and no sign-up. Your projects are kept on your own computer.

From the first idea to a story people can play

Arcanum follows the way a story gets made. Move between the stages whenever you like, and press Play story at any moment to see it as a reader will.

1 · Draft

Plan on a board of cards

Lay out your story as cards. Draw branching choices as links between them, add stop points, and watch a glowing trace follow the path while you play it.

2 · Write

Write it as steps, a script or a manuscript

The same story in three views. The manuscript has chapters, notes, revision colours, a dialogue view and a writing history. Bring in a Word, text or Markdown document you've already written.

3 · Produce

Bring in your art

Pages, backgrounds, characters and their expressions. Cut scenes from a comic page, build layers, design your title screen, and keep track of what's left to do.

4 · Stage

Stage each scene on a timeline

Camera moves, overlays, music and sound, timed to your lines. Save a scene as a video when you want to share a moment.

5 · Publish

Share a story anyone can read

Check for dead ends with the story checker, see every branch on a map, then publish the story as a single web page. Your readers need nothing but a browser.

Your work stays on your computer

Arcanum has no accounts and no servers. Nothing you make is uploaded, even when you use it here on the website.

Saved as you go

Every change is kept in your browser on this computer, so your projects are waiting next time you open the studio.

Save a project file

File ▸ Save project file puts a copy on your computer. In Chrome and Edge you choose the folder; other browsers use your Downloads folder.

Open it anywhere

File ▸ Load project file opens that copy again: on another computer, in the desktop app, or after clearing your browser.

Clearing your browser's history and site data also clears the projects kept in it. Save a project file now and then, and tick the box to include pictures and sounds when you want a complete copy.

Windows or Mac says the app can't be opened

The installers aren't signed with a paid certificate, so your computer asks once. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, right-click Arcanum in Applications, choose Open, then Open again (or go to System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and press Open Anyway).

How the single file works

Arcanum.html is the whole studio in one file. Save it anywhere and double-click it to open it in your browser, even with no internet. Projects are kept in that browser on that computer, just like the website. Keep the file in one place, because moving it starts a fresh, empty workspace.
